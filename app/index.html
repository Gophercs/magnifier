Test the buzzIf you feel nothing, check the phone's Vibration & haptics settings

  
Camera readingsShows what the camera says it's doing (for tracking down bugs)

  
Light off while frozenSwitches the light off when you freeze, and back on when you return to the camera

  
Focus stackingIn manual focus, the shutter takes 5 pictures from 2 cm nearer to 2 cm further, lines them up, and keeps the sharpest parts of each. Then you can flick through them and save any.

  
Full-resolution photosTakes a proper 12 MP still after freezing. Slower, and may flicker the light.
